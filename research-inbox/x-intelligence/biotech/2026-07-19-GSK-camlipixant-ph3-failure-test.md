## Event

GSK announced termination of camlipixant development on July 17, 2026. Camlipixant is a P2X3 receptor antagonist developed for refractory chronic cough (RCC). GSK acquired it from Bellus Health in 2023 for approximately $2B.

Phase 3 program: two pivotal studies. One study met the primary endpoint (reduction in cough frequency). The second study failed the same endpoint. On these mixed results, GSK elected to discontinue the program entirely.

Five monitored journalist accounts (adamfeuerstein, FierceBiotech, endpts, BioPharmaDive, statnews) all posted July 17 within a ~2-hour window, with @adamfeuerstein's post reaching 36,842 impressions and 50 likes — the highest-engagement biotech post in the sweep window.

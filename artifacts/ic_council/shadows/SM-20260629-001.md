## Signal or claim

DEM frozen Top-30 shows selection-driven YTD excess vs random same-universe baskets (99.1st percentile, 2000 bootstrap controls, Jan 2 – Jun 18 2026, gross of costs). Pre-2025 cross-sectional signal established (t_nw=2.795, alpha stream beta=0.05, 55 periods). 2025+ rally-concentrated excess raises the question of whether forward selection skill persists.

## Why not approved

- In-sample only (single YTD window, 5.5 months)
- Gross of costs
- 2025+ alpha stream beta=0.52 (rally-participation, not cross-sectional)
- Rally cluster (5 months) accounts for 83.5% of 2025+ cumulative excess
- 2025+ ex-rally residual t_nw=1.433 is sub-threshold and has only 9 observations

## Why not rejected

- Pre-2025 cross-sectional signal well-established: 55 periods, t_nw=2.795, alpha stream beta=0.05
- Bootstrap places model at 99.1%ile vs 2000 size-matched random baskets (p=0.009, z=2.51)
- Model is positive in all three regimes (bull t=2.055, bear t=1.954, neutral t=2.688)
- 118 weekly forward captures already accumulated; forward bootstrap is computable now
- 2025+ ex-rally residual (t=1.433) is sub-threshold but not noise — insufficient observations to confirm or refute

## Hypothesis

Frozen Top-30 (weekly snapshots) will continue to outperform random same-universe baskets on forward captures, net of costs, across both rally and non-rally regimes.

## Primary metric

Forward bootstrap percentile: for each weekly snapshot, compare model Top-30 5d forward return to 2000 random same-universe baskets drawn with matching methodology. Report percentile distribution across all accumulated windows.

## Secondary metrics

- Regime-stratified forward bootstrap percentile (rally vs non-rally separately)
- Net-of-cost forward excess vs XBI (requires cost estimate per basis point)
- EES exclusion sensitivity (rerun with EES-flagged names excluded)

## Comparison group

2000 random EW Top-30 baskets drawn from the same eligible universe at each weekly snapshot date; same 5d forward holding window; same price source.

## Evaluation window

- **Type:** MODEL_EVALUATION
- **Start:** 2026-01-02 (first weekly capture in forward monitor)
- **Due:** 2026-09-30 (operator to confirm)
- **Basis:** Success/failure can be assessed once forward bootstrap percentile is computed; 118 captures already available. The 2026-09-30 due date gives time for the forward bootstrap script to be written, run, and reviewed.

## Success threshold

Forward bootstrap percentile ≥75th percentile across the full capture series, with positive net excess (net of estimated transaction costs), evaluated across ≥20 completed windows. Rally and non-rally percentiles should both be >50 for the cross-sectional claim to hold.

## Failure threshold

Forward bootstrap percentile ≤50th percentile after ≥20 completed windows, or negative net excess. If the non-rally percentile is ≤50 while the rally percentile is high, the finding is rally-participation only — not investable as a regime-agnostic model.

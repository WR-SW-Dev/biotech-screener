# Biotech M&A Landscape — April 2025 through March 2026

## 2025 Summary
- Total deal value: ~$133-143B across ~129 deals (2x YoY)
- No mega-deals >$30B but multiple $10B+ transactions
- Shift toward late-stage/commercial assets, $5-15B sweet spot
- Drivers: patent cliffs (Keytruda), pipeline replenishment, strong balance sheets

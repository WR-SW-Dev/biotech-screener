# Catalyst Event Probability Reasoning Checklist

### A. Base Rate Anchoring
1. **Is the base rate of successful biotech catalyst events within your investment horizon historically low?**
2. **Do you understand the typical timeline for regulatory approvals and market reactions to new therapies or technologies?**
3. **Have you accounted for the high variability in clinical trial outcomes, which can be influenced by factors like patient recruitment rates and study design?**
4. **Are you aware of the potential for black swan events that could dramatically alter the base rate of success in biotech?**

### B. Trial Design Quality
1. **Is the clinical trial well-designed with a sufficient sample size to detect meaningful differences?**
2. **Does the trial protocol include appropriate endpoints and statistical significance thresholds?**
3. **Are there potential biases or limitations in the study design that could affect the validity of results?**
4. **Have you considered whether the trial is being conducted by reputable institutions with track records for successful clinical trials?**

### C. Bayesian Updates from Data Signals
1. **Do you update your probability estimates based on interim data and statistical analyses as they become available?**
2. **Are you incorporating feedback from peer-reviewed literature, expert opinions, and market sentiment into your assessments?**
3. **Have you considered the possibility of multiple hypotheses being tested simultaneously in a single trial?**
4. **Do you account for the potential for publication bias or selective reporting that could skew the interpretation of results?**

### D. Market Calibration vs Model
1. **Is there a significant discrepancy between market expectations and your quantitative model predictions?**
2. **Have you considered alternative explanations for market movements, such as macroeconomic factors or geopolitical events?**
3. **Do you regularly calibrate your models against historical data to ensure they remain relevant and accurate?**
4. **Are you aware of potential feedback loops where market sentiment influences trial outcomes, which in turn affect market perception?**

### E. Position Sizing and EV
1. **Is the expected value (EV) of the catalyst event sufficiently high to justify your position size?**
2. **Do you have a risk management plan that considers the potential for downside scenarios?**
3. **Are you aware of the limitations in using EV as a sole metric, recognizing that it does not account for market dynamics and investor psychology?**
4. **Have you considered whether the catalyst event is part of a larger portfolio strategy or if it stands alone as an investment opportunity?**

### F. Post-Event Review
1. **Do you conduct a thorough review of the event outcomes to understand what went right and wrong?**
2. **Are you learning from past mistakes and adjusting your approach for future events?**
3. **Have you considered whether the market's reaction was justified based on the available data at the time?**
4. **Do you have a system in place for continuously updating your models and assumptions as new information becomes available?**
